## What is Kharchafy?

Kharchafy is an expense tracker and digital khata app (an online khata book for your hisaab kitab) for friends, roommates, families, students and travelers. It keeps group expenses, shared loans (udhaar / qarz), personal expenses, personal loans, salary and budgets, receipts, approvals and notifications in one place, and turns them into PDF or Excel reports whenever you need them.

It is built for how people in Pakistan actually manage money: amounts in PKR (and other currencies), Urdu and English side by side, and group records that nobody can quietly change. If you are looking for a **Splitwise alternative** or a **Khatabook alternative** that speaks Urdu, this is it.

- **Web app, installable app (PWA) and Android app**, all with one account.
- **English and اردو**, switchable any time, with a full right-to-left Urdu layout.
- **Free to sign up and use.** AI receipt and voice entry come with a monthly allowance.

## What do you want to do?

### Split bills with friends, roommates or on a trip
Add an expense once and Kharchafy works out who paid, who owes and how much: equal or custom splits, one payer or several. It suits roommate rent and utility bills, trips, family expenses, hostel mess bills, office lunches and events. At the end of the month, settlement shows exactly who pays whom so the group can settle up.
➡️ [Group expense tracker](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/group-expense-tracker)

### Keep the hisaab of udhaar and shared loans
Record money given and received between group members (udhaar, qarz), mark repayments as paid and have the other person confirm them, and settle a month once everything is repaid. Private loans with people outside a group stay in your own personal loans list.
➡️ [Features](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/features)

### Track daily and monthly kharcha against your salary
Write down roz ka kharcha as it happens, plan a monthly budget by category, set a savings goal, and see how much of your salary is left. History shows how each month compared.
➡️ [Personal budget tracker](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/personal-budget-tracker)

### Add expenses faster from a receipt or your voice
Take a photo of a receipt, or just say what you spent, and the form fills itself in. You check every field before saving, so a misread never becomes a record.
➡️ [Features](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/features)

## Features

### Group expense tracker
- Create group expenses for trips, roommates, family bills and events.
- Choose the payer (or several payers), members, category, date, amount, description and receipt.
- See who paid, who owes and how every shared balance changed.

### Shared loans and settlements
- Track money given and received between group members.
- Keep shared loans separate from regular shared expenses for cleaner records.
- Mark payments as paid, have the receiver confirm them, and close the month with a settlement everyone approves.
- Settled months stay in the group's History for 60 days, with a download link.

### Personal expenses, salary and budgets
- Record monthly salary, daily expenses, categories, locations, recipients and notes.
- Plan a monthly budget by category and set a savings goal, with optional AI suggestions.
- Watch total spending, remaining balance and month-by-month history.

### Personal loans
- Track private loans you gave or received without mixing them into group balances.
- Download detailed loan reports when you need a personal record.

### Approvals, receipts and notifications
- Edits and deletions of shared records need every member's approval, so nobody changes the group's numbers alone.
- Attach receipt images where proof is useful later.
- Get in-app, email and push notifications for anything waiting on you.
- Save your JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet details so group members know where to pay you.

### Currencies, languages and devices
- Each group keeps one currency (PKR or any other), and you can enter an expense in whatever currency you paid in.
- Switch between English and اردو at any time.
- If your connection drops mid-entry, what you typed is kept on your device until you save it.

### Reports
- Download PDF or Excel reports for shared expenses, shared loans, personal expenses and personal loans.
- Review summaries, categories, participants, dates and descriptions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Kharchafy free?**
Yes. You can sign up and use it for free. AI receipt scanning, voice entry and budget suggestions come with a monthly allowance.

**Is Kharchafy available in Urdu?**
Yes. The whole app, including help and the legal pages, is available in Urdu with a right-to-left layout. [Open it in Urdu](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/ur).

**Is there a Kharchafy Android app?**
Yes. Download it from the [download page](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/download). You can also install the web app on any phone or computer from your browser.

**Can I use Kharchafy as a Splitwise or Khatabook alternative?**
Yes. It splits bills and settles up like Splitwise, keeps an udhaar khata like Khatabook, and adds Urdu, PKR, personal budgets, approvals for shared changes, and PDF/Excel reports.

**How do I split bills with roommates?**
Create a group, invite your roommates, and add each bill with who paid. Kharchafy keeps everyone's balance up to date and shows who owes whom at the end of the month. See the [group expense tracker](https://kharchafy-khata-application.vercel.app/group-expense-tracker).

**How do I keep track of udhaar (money I lent)?**
Add it as a shared loan in a group, or as a personal loan if the other person isn't in one. Repayments are recorded as they happen, so the remaining amount is always clear.

**Which currencies does it support?**
PKR and other major currencies. Expenses entered in another currency are converted at the day's rate.
